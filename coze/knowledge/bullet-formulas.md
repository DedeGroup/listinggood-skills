# Amazon Bullet Point Generator Reference — Formulas & Examples

Comprehensive reference for writing high-converting Amazon bullet points, with category-specific examples and before/after comparisons.

## The Feature-to-Benefit Translation Engine

The core skill of bullet writing: **turn every feature into a customer benefit**.

| Raw Feature | Bad Bullet (feature-only) | Good Bullet (benefit-led) |
|-------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| "Cotton material" | "Made of cotton" | "**BREATHABLE COMFORT** — 100% premium cotton keeps you cool and dry all day, no more sticky discomfort in warm weather" |
| "5000mAh battery" | "Has a 5000mAh battery" | "**ALL-DAY POWER** — 5000mAh battery provides up to 18 hours of talk time or 72 hours of standby, so you're never caught hunting for an outlet" |
| "Non-slip sole" | "The sole is non-slip" | "**SURE-FOOTED GRIP** — Textured rubber outsole with deep lugs grips wet and uneven surfaces, giving you stable footing from morning commute to weekend trail" |
| "Dishwasher safe" | "It is dishwasher safe" | "**EFFORTLESS CLEANUP** — Pop it straight into the dishwasher (top or bottom rack) — no scrubbing, no soaking, more time for what matters" |

## Header Formula Library

### Benefit-Led Headers (Default Framework)
Use these templates by filling in the bracketed part:

**For comfort/wearability:**
- **[TIME DURATION] COMFORT** — e.g., "ALL-DAY COMFORT", "24-HOUR WEAR"
- **[ADJECTIVE] FIT** — e.g., "CUSTOMIZABLE FIT", "SECURE FIT"
- **FEELS LIKE [POSITIVE THING]** — e.g., "FEELS LIKE A SECOND SKIN"

**For quality/durability:**
- **BUILT TO [TIME/RESULT]** — e.g., "BUILT TO LAST A LIFETIME"
- **PREMIUM [MATERIAL/CRAFTSMANSHIP]** — e.g., "PREMIUM CRAFTSMANSHIP"
- **[COMPARISON] DURABILITY** — e.g., "3X THE LONGEVITY"

**For versatility/use cases:**
- **DESIGNED FOR [USE CASE]** — e.g., "DESIGNED FOR EVERYDAY ADVENTURE"
- **ONE [ITEM], [NUMBER] USES** — e.g., "ONE BAG, FIVE WAYS TO WEAR"
- **FROM [SCENARIO A] TO [SCENARIO B]** — e.g., "FROM OFFICE TO OUTDOORS"

**For uniqueness/differentiation:**
- **EXCLUSIVE [FEATURE NAME]** — e.g., "EXCLUSIVE CLIMATE CONTROL"
- **PATENTED / PROPRIETARY [TECH]** — e.g., "PROPRIETARY FLOW TECHNOLOGY"
- **THE ONLY [ITEM] THAT [DOES X]** — e.g., "THE ONLY PACK THAT CONVERTS INTO A DUFFEL"

**For trust/peace of mind:**
- **BUY WITH CONFIDENCE** — (classic closer)
- **BACKED BY [GUARANTEE/WARRANTY]** — e.g., "BACKED BY OUR 2-YEAR WARRANTY"
- **[BRAND NAME] PROMISE** — e.g., "THE LISTINGGOOD PROMISE"

## Proof Point Types to Include

Every bullet should have at least one proof point. Use this checklist:

| Type | Example | Impact |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Number** | "lasts 3x longer" | High — specific = credible |
| **Material** | "genuine full-grain leather" | Medium-High — tangible quality signal |
| **Certification** | "OEKO-TEX Standard 100" | High — third-party validation |
| **Time duration** | "keeps drinks cold for 24 hours" | High — sets clear expectation |
| **Comparison** | "15% thicker than standard" | Medium-High — contextualizes value |
| **Result/outcome** | "charges iPhone 14 to 50% in 30 min" | Very High — user can visualize benefit |
| **Guarantee/warranty** | "2-year no-questions warranty" | High — removes risk |

## Character Count Guide

| Length | Readability | Best For |
|--------|------------|----------|
| 100–200 chars | ✅ Excellent — scannable | Mobile-first products |
| 200–300 chars | ✅✅ Ideal — detailed but readable | Most categories (default target) |
| 300–400 chars | ⚠️ Acceptable — thorough | Technical/complex products |
| 400–500 chars | ⚠️ Risky — many shoppers won't read | Only if info is critical |
| 500+ chars | ❌ Will be truncated | Avoid — split into two bullets |

## The "Skim Test"

After writing all 5 bullets, do this test:
1. Read **only the bold headers** in sequence → Does the story make sense?
2. A shopper who reads **only headers** should understand: what it is, why buy it, and why trust it
3. If headers alone don't tell the story → rewrite headers to be more specific
